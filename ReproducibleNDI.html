ReproducibleNDI

Three neighborhood indices from 5-year ACS data, following the ndi package by Ian D. Buller. computeNDI() builds the Messer and Powell-Wiley deprivation indices, which use principal component analysis. computeICE() builds Krieger's Index of Concentration at the Extremes, which uses ratios instead.

Messer NDI Powell-Wiley NDI ICE
Call computeNDI(type = "messer") computeNDI(type = "powell_wiley") computeICE()

Scope: national | state | county · Geography: county | tract

Standardization, PCA and scoring run separately within each scope group. Block group isn't available because the ACS data-profile (DP04) and subject (S1501, S1702, S2301) tables stop at tract level.

Index of Concentration at the Extremes

computeICE()

No scope · no ranking · no PCA

Each measure is a ratio computed separately for each geography, (Advantaged − Deprived) / Total, ranging from −1 (deprived) to +1 (privileged). No geographies are ranked or pooled, so there is no scope argument.

1

Fetch ACS Data

fetchICEAcs()

ACS-5 detailed tables: B19001 · B19001H · B19001B · B15002 · B03002, down to block group. Names split by cleanAcsNames()

2

Compute 5 ICE Measures

calculateICEVars()

Income · education · race/ethnicity · racialized economic · White income. Income cutpoints change in 2016. Non-finite → NA

Optional
3

Impute

imputeMissingICE()

Spatial mean over Queen-contiguity neighbors. Each measure is imputed on its own (unweighted)

4

Assemble Output

computeICE()

Returns df_ice, the component sums, geometry and a missingness summary per measure

ICE_inchigh-income − low-income households
ICE_edubachelor's+ − less than high school (25+)
ICE_rewbnon-Hispanic White − non-Hispanic Black
ICE_wbinchigh-income White − low-income Black households
ICE_wpcinchigh-income − low-income White households
  • Block group is supported because every input comes from a detailed B-table. ndi::krieger stops at tract.
  • Cutpoints: before 2016, low income is < $25k and high is > $100k. From 2016 on, low is < $30k and high is > $125k.

Krieger N et al. 2016, Am J Public Health 106(2):256–263 · ported from ndi::krieger

Every index returns an annotation per geography: Complete data · Imputation completed · Imputation incomplete · PCA failed · No population. The ICE annotation uses only the first three. With impute = TRUE, the result also includes a baseline without imputation (df_ndi_raw / df_ice_raw) for side-by-side comparison.
