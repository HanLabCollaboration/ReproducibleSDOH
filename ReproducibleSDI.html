ReproducibleSDI

The Social Deprivation Index (Robert Graham Center) combines 7 ACS measures of neighborhood deprivation with a single-factor analysis. computeSDI() builds it from the Census API.

Butler et al. 2013

Social Deprivation Index

computeSDI()

Scope: national | state

Imputation, rescaling and factor analysis run separately within each scope group. "state" scope takes effect only when more than one state is requested. For 2011–2012, only county and tract are available.

1

Fetch ACS Data

fetchSDIAcs()

ACS-5 detailed tables: B01003 · B17001 · B15002 · B23001 · B25003 · B25014 · B11003 · B25044. Names split by cleanAcsNamesSDI()

2

Compute 7 Variables

calculateSDIVars()

7 percentages, each converted to a population-weighted centile (1–100) across all fetched geographies

Optional
3

Impute

imputeMissing()

Spatial mean over Queen-contiguity neighbors. Off by default for SDI

4

Rescale

scale() · rank()

Complete cases only. z-score (default) or rank each centile within the scope group

5

Factor Analysis

psych::fa()

1 factor, principal axis, weighted by population. Bartlett scores give the SDI

6

Centile Score

processSDIScope()

our_SDI = population-weighted centile (1–100) of the SDI score
